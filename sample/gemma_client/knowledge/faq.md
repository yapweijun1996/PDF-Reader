## What is ScreenClip Pro?
ScreenClip Pro is described on the public GitHub repository as a browser-side screen recorder and editor that captures a display, trims the recording, and exports MP4 entirely on the client using FFmpeg.wasm.

## What is PayNow QR Generator?
PayNow QR Generator is a public web app that generates PayNow QR codes from payment information such as UEN, amount, expiry date, reference number, and company name.
